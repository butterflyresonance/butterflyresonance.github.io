Your details & targets
Drift up to the top of the band, then trim back to the bottom, then repeat. Every year or two, move the whole band up by a couple of kilos as you actually add tissue.
Slower gaining puts on a better ratio of muscle to fat. Faster trimming costs you muscle. The defaults are conservative on both counts.
The waist estimate reads a few points high on muscular builds, because it can't tell a thick torso from a fat one. If you ever get a DEXA or callipers done, switch to entering it yourself.

History

Trend maths: weight is smoothed with a 7-day half-life and the rate of change comes from a recency-weighted fit over the last 28 days. Waist uses a 10-day half-life, with your food rating subtracted first — the app starts with an assumption of about 0.4 cm per point and then learns your own figure once you've logged enough rated days. Energy adjustments assume roughly 7,700 kcal per kilo, which is a rule of thumb, not a law. This is a bookkeeping tool, not medical advice.
