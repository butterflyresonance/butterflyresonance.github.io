Fine tuning
The fat-per-cm figure converts waist movement into kilos of fat, so it drives the muscle-or-fat split. Around 1.0 kg/cm is typical for men and 0.8 for women. Two DEXA scans a few months apart would let you work out your own.

History

Two different models are at work here, deliberately. The body fat percentage comes from Relative Fat Mass, a static formula using waist and height — it sets your weight range and reads a few points high on muscular builds. The muscle-or-fat split uses a separate figure, kilograms of fat per centimetre of waist, because a static formula makes a poor model of change. They can disagree; the split is the one to trust for day-to-day decisions. Weight is smoothed with a 7-day half-life, waist with 10 days, and rates come from a recency-weighted fit over the last 28 days. Energy adjustments assume roughly 7,700 kcal per kilo, a rule of thumb rather than a law. This is a bookkeeping tool, not medical advice.
