How this works

No account to make, and the game is kept only on your two devices. While you both have the page open, each device stays connected to a small table server, which hands each move to the other device as it’s made. The server keeps nothing: it only passes moves along. It’s closer to a phone call than to posting letters through an office.

The catch is the same as a phone call: you both have to be there. A move only crosses while both pages are open. Play one and close the tab before the other person looks, and it’ll wait and go through next time you’re both on.

Your own device remembers the position, so refreshing or coming back tomorrow picks up where you left off.
