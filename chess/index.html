Play chess with one other person. Pick a side, send them the link, and you’re both looking at the same board.

How this works

There’s nothing in the middle. No account to make, nothing kept on a website somewhere — while you both have the page open, your two devices find each other and pass moves straight between them. It’s closer to a phone call than to posting letters through an office.

The catch is the same as a phone call: you both have to be there. A move only crosses while both pages are open. Play one and close the tab before the other person looks, and it’ll wait and go through next time you’re both on.

Your own device remembers the position, so refreshing or coming back tomorrow picks up where you left off.
