About the Practice

Coordinated care across multiple specialties

More Than Medicine is a specialist medical consulting space in the heart of Canberra, formed by clinicians with a shared commitment to quality, patient-centred care across a range of medical disciplines.

Our model brings specialists together in a coordinated environment — reducing the burden on patients and their families while ensuring continuity across complex or multi-system conditions. All investigations are available at the request of your General Practitioner or another specialist.

Our Specialties

Expert care across five disciplines

🫀
Cardiology
Adult Congenital Heart Disease, Pulmonary Hypertension, cardiac imaging, arrhythmia, heart failure, and maternal cardiology with access to visiting consultants from RPA Sydney.
🧠
Geriatric Medicine
Comprehensive geriatric assessment, cognitive impairment, falls, osteoporosis, polypharmacy, capacity assessments, and complex aged care navigation. Home and nursing home visits available.
🫁
Respiratory Medicine
Complex lung disease, lung transplantation assessment, and full respiratory function testing. Dr Pearson also works part-time with the Lung Transplantation Unit at St Vincent's, Sydney.
💧
Nephrology
Renal transplantation, obstetric nephrology, pre-eclampsia, blood pressure disorders of pregnancy, and general nephrology including dialysis management.
🩺
General Medicine
Broad physician care across complex or multi-system presentations, with coordinated support from across the practice.
👩‍⚕️
Nurse Practitioner
Specialist nursing care in aged care, cognitive and capacity assessments, stomal therapy, continence advice, and aged care portal navigation for families and healthcare professionals.
